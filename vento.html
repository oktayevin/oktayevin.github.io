Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vento work?

Vento uses a step-based reward engine to convert your daily movement into valuable in-app coins. You can use these coins to support campaigns, donate to causes, or convert them directly to cash.

Is Vento safe to use?

Yes, Vento is a secure platform. We use advanced security measures to protect your data and transactions. Your funds are also held in a secure escrow account until the campaign is complete.

How long does it take to receive payments?

Payments are processed quickly and securely. The exact time it takes depends on the payment method you choose. For credit card payments, it usually takes 1-3 business days for the funds to appear in your account.

Can I use Vento for both personal and business purposes?

Yes, Vento is designed to be business-friendly. You can use it for both personal and business purposes. Whether you're supporting a cause you care about or earning money for your startup, Vento has you covered.
