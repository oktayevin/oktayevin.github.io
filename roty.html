How it works

Simple loop.
Earn → Spend → Repeat.

01
Pick your daily quests
Choose from 50+ built-in habits — reading, working out, drinking water, brushing teeth, and more. Pick the ones you want to build.
💪 Habits
02
Complete them & earn 🪙 coins
Start a timer, snap a photo as proof, or tap to confirm. Harder quests and longer timers reward more coins. Streaks keep the momentum.
🪙 Coins
03
Spend coins to unlock apps
Head to the Shop and pick how many minutes you want. Roty uses Apple's Screen Time API to unblock your chosen apps instantly.
🔓 Unlock
04
Apps re-lock when time's up
When the clock runs out, your apps are blocked again — automatically, in the background. Want more time? Go earn more coins. No shortcuts.

Quest Types

Three ways to
prove it.

⏱
Focus · Timer Quest
Stay on task
Set a duration and start. Roty tracks it live — even in the background. Commit longer = earn more coins. Works for reading, studying, gym, yoga and more.
📸
Proof · Photo Quest
Show the receipts
Snap a photo and AI verifies it. Made your bed? Drank water? Ate a fruit? Took out the trash? Roty checks if the photo matches the quest — no cheating.
✅
Honor · Statement Quest
Your word is enough
For habits that can't be measured — slept 8 hours, took vitamins, called a friend, said thank you. Tap to confirm. Roty trusts you. Don't break the streak.
