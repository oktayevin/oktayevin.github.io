Who We Are

Roty is an iOS application developed and published by Oktay Evin, an independent developer based in Istanbul, Turkey. Roty is a gamified habit-building app that uses Apple's Screen Time API to let users lock and unlock apps based on coins earned by completing real-life daily quests.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Roty iOS app and governs how any information related to your use of the App is handled. By using Roty, you agree to the practices described in this document.

📬

For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at hello@oktayevin.com

02
⚡
The Short Version

If you only read one section, read this one:

All your data lives on your device. Roty has no backend server. There is no database we control that contains your information.

We never transmit personal data. Your name, quests, coins, streaks, and habits never leave your iPhone.

No analytics, no trackers, no ads. Roty contains zero third-party SDKs that collect behavioral or personal data.

No account required. You never sign up, log in, or provide an email address to use Roty.

Deleting the app deletes everything. Uninstalling Roty removes all locally stored data permanently.

Photos taken for photo quests are never saved or stored. When you complete a photo quest, the photo is passed directly to on-device image recognition and immediately discarded. It is never written to your Photo Library, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere.

04
🚫
Data We Do Not Collect

To be completely explicit, Roty never collects, processes, or transmits any of the following:

Device identifiers — no IDFA, IDFV, serial number, or any hardware identifier

Location data — Roty never requests or reads your GPS location

IP address or network data — Roty makes no outbound network requests during normal use

Contacts, calendar, or messages — Roty does not access any communication data

Crash reports or diagnostic data — no crash reporting SDK is included

Behavioral analytics — no events, sessions, screen views, or tap data are tracked

Financial or payment information — in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Roty never sees your payment details

Health data beyond step count — Roty only reads today's step count from HealthKit and does not read, write, or store any other health metrics

05
🔗
Third-Party Services

Roty contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, or data brokers. The only external services involved are Apple's own platform services:

Apple HealthKit — read-only, fetches your daily step count. Governed by Apple's privacy policy and iOS permissions you control.

Apple Screen Time (FamilyControls / ManagedSettings / DeviceActivity) — used to block and unblock your chosen apps. Data stays entirely within iOS.

Apple Push Notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter) — local, on-device notifications only (e.g. timer expiry alerts). No remote push server.

Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles any in-app purchase transactions. Roty only receives a purchase confirmation, never your payment details.

Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/privacy.

06
📱
Apple Platform Permissions

Roty requests the following iOS permissions. You can revoke any at any time in iOS Settings → Roty.

Screen Time (FamilyControls) — Required. Used for app blocking and unlocking via the DeviceActivityMonitor extension. The core feature won't work without this.

Apple Health (HealthKit — Steps only) — Optional. Reads today's step count for the step reward feature. Only step count is accessed, no other health data.

Notifications — Optional. Used for local, on-device reminders only (e.g. relock alerts). No remote push notifications whatsoever.

Camera — Optional. Used only during photo quest verification. Photos are processed on-device and immediately discarded — never saved or shared.

✅

Roty will always explain why a permission is needed before requesting it. You will never be surprised by an unexpected permission prompt.

07
🗑
Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are always in full control of it.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored Roty data permanently — quest history, coin balance, streak, name, preferences — everything.

Revoking HealthKit permission in iOS Settings immediately stops Roty from reading step data. Previously read step counts are not stored and are discarded after display.

There is no account to delete — we hold no copy of your data on any server. No deletion request needs to be submitted to us.

Children's Privacy

Roty is designed to be suitable for users of all ages. The onboarding flow explicitly supports users as young as 8 years old.

Because Roty collects no personal data and transmits nothing off-device, it does not raise concerns under children's privacy regulations including COPPA (US), GDPR-K (EU), or KVKK (Turkey). No personal information is collected, processed, or shared regardless of the user's age.

⚠️

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about your child's use of Roty, please contact us at hello@oktayevin.com. We are happy to assist.

Your Rights

Because Roty stores all data locally and we hold no personal data on our end, most rights are automatically satisfied — you already have full access, control, portability, and erasure capability through your own device.

Right to access — All your data is on your device. Open the app to see it at any time.

Right to rectification — Edit your name and settings directly inside the app's Settings screen.

Right to erasure — Delete the app. All data is gone immediately. No server-side copy exists.

Right to portability — Your data is in standard iOS formats, included in any iPhone backup you choose to create.

Right to object / restrict — Revoke any individual permission (Health, Notifications, Camera) at any time via iOS Settings without deleting the app.

If you believe your rights have not been satisfied, contact us at hello@oktayevin.com. EU residents may also lodge a complaint with their local Data Protection Authority. Turkish residents may contact the KVKK at kvkk.gov.tr.
