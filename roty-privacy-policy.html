Who We Are

Roty is an iOS application developed and published by Oktay Evin, an independent developer based in Istanbul, Turkey. Roty is a personal productivity and habit-building app that uses Apple's Screen Time API to let users lock and unlock apps based on earned in-app coins.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Roty iOS app ("the App") and governs how any information related to your use of the App is handled. By using Roty, you agree to the practices described in this document.

📬
For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at hello@oktayevin.com

The Short Version

If you only read one section, read this one:

All your data lives on your device. Roty does not have a backend server. There is no database we control that contains your information.
We never transmit personal data. Your name, age, step count, quest history, coins, and habits never leave your iPhone.
No analytics, no trackers, no ads. Roty contains zero third-party SDKs that collect behavioral or personal data.
No account required. You never sign up, log in, or provide an email address to use Roty.
Deleting the app deletes everything. Uninstalling Roty removes all locally stored data from your device permanently.

Data We Collect & Why

Roty stores a minimal set of data locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (SwiftData and UserDefaults). None of it is accessible to us or any third party.

```
Data Where it's stored Why it's needed
Your first name (optional, entered during onboarding) On-device (UserDefaults) Personalises the "Hello, [Name]" greeting in the app
Age & daily phone usage estimate (entered during onboarding) On-device (UserDefaults) Calculates the brain "rot meter" and impact stats shown in onboarding
Quest list and completion history On-device (SwiftData) Tracks which quests you've completed today and your overall progress
Coin balance On-device (UserDefaults) Stores your earned coins so you can spend them in the Shop
Daily streak count On-device (UserDefaults) Tracks consecutive days of completing at least one quest
Step count (read-only, from Apple Health) Never stored — read live from HealthKit Displays today's steps and calculates bonus coin rewards
Selected apps for Screen Time blocking On-device App Group (shared container) Tells the Screen Time extension which apps to block/unblock
App unlock timer state On-device (UserDefaults) Tracks whether apps are currently unlocked and how much time remains
Onboarding completion flag On-device (UserDefaults) Remembers whether you've completed setup so it's not shown again
App theme preference On-device (UserDefaults) Remembers your light/dark/system appearance setting

Photos taken for photo quests are never saved or stored. When you complete a photo quest (e.g., "Drink Water", "Make Bed"), the photo is passed directly to on-device image recognition and immediately discarded. It is never written to your Photo Library, never saved to disk, and never transmitted anywhere.
```

Data We Do Not Collect

To be completely explicit, Roty never collects, processes, or transmits any of the following:

Device identifiers — no IDFA, IDFV, serial number, or any hardware identifier
Location data — Roty never requests or reads your GPS location
IP address or network data — Roty makes no outbound network requests during normal use
Contacts, calendar, or messages — Roty does not access any communication data
Crash reports or diagnostic data — no crash reporting SDK is included
Behavioral analytics — no events, sessions, screen views, or tap data are tracked
Financial or payment information — in-app purchases (if applicable) are handled entirely by Apple. Roty never sees your payment details
Health data beyond step count — Roty only reads today's step count from HealthKit and does not read, write, or store any other health metrics

🔗
Third-Party Services

Roty contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporters, or data brokers. The only external services involved in your experience with Roty are Apple's own platform services:

Apple HealthKit — used in read-only mode to fetch your daily step count. Governed by Apple's privacy policy and the permissions you grant in iOS Settings.
Apple Screen Time (FamilyControls / ManagedSettings / DeviceActivity) — used to block and unblock your chosen apps. This data stays entirely within iOS and is never accessible to Roty's code once the blocking is applied.
Apple Push Notifications (UNUserNotificationCenter) — used only to send local, on-device notifications (e.g., when your unlock timer is about to expire). Roty does not use remote push notifications and has no push notification server.
Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles any in-app purchase transactions. Apple's privacy practices govern all payment processing. Roty only receives a confirmation of purchase status, never your payment details.

Apple's privacy policy is available at apple.com/privacy.

06
📱
Apple Platform Permissions

Roty requests the following iOS permissions. All are optional except Screen Time, which is core to the app's functionality. You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings → Roty.

Screen Time (FamilyControls) — Required for app blocking and unlocking. Roty uses the DeviceActivityMonitor extension to apply and remove app shields. Without this permission, the core unlock feature does not work.
Apple Health (HealthKit — Steps read-only) — Optional. Used to read today's step count for the step reward feature. Roty only reads step count and no other health data.
Notifications — Optional. Used for local, on-device reminders only (e.g., app relock alerts). No remote notifications. Notification content is generated entirely on your device.
Camera — Optional, used only during photo quest verification. Photos are processed on-device for content recognition and immediately discarded. They are never saved, shared, or stored.
✅
Roty will always explain why a permission is needed before requesting it. You will never be surprised by an unexpected permission prompt.

Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you are always in full control of it.

Deleting the app removes all locally stored Roty data from your device permanently, including your quest history, coin balance, streak, name, and preferences.
Revoking HealthKit permission in iOS Settings will immediately stop Roty from reading any step data. Previously read step counts are not stored by Roty and are discarded after display.
There is no account to delete because there is no account. We hold no copy of your data on any server. No deletion request needs to be submitted to us.

If you have questions about data specific to Apple's own platform services (iCloud backups, HealthKit, App Store receipts), those are managed through your Apple ID settings.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have specific legal rights regarding your personal data. Because Roty stores all data locally on your device and we do not process or hold any personal data on our end, most of these rights are automatically satisfied — you already have full access, control, portability, and erasure capability through your own device.

Right to access — All your data is on your device. Open the app to see it at any time.
Right to rectification — You can edit your name and settings directly inside the app's Settings screen.
Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — Delete the app. All data is gone immediately. There is no server-side copy to delete.
Right to data portability — Your data is stored in standard iOS formats. iPhone backups (via iCloud or iTunes) will include it if you choose to back up your device.
Right to object / restrict processing — You can revoke any individual permission (Health, Notifications, Camera) at any time via iOS Settings without deleting the app.

If you believe your rights have not been satisfied, you may contact us at hello@oktayevin.com. Residents of the EU may also lodge a complaint with their local Data Protection Authority. Residents of Turkey may contact the Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu (KVKK) at kvkk.gov.tr.
